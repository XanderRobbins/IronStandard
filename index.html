06Questions

Before you apply

Anything not covered here, ask on your intro call.

Do I need to be in great shape to start?

No. Every program is built around where you are today. If you can commit to showing up, we’ll scale the training to your level.

Do I need a gym?

Not necessarily. Tell me what equipment you have access to on your application and I’ll build the plan around it, whether that’s a full gym, a garage setup, or just dumbbells.

Is this only for men who are already strong in their faith?

No. Whether you’re rock solid, rusty, or just getting started, you’re welcome. The teaching meets you where you are.

What does the nutrition part include?

Suggested calorie, protein, and nutrient target ranges that fit your goals and your life. It’s general guidance, not rigid meal plans or medical diet advice.

What happens on the weekly call?

Thirty minutes to review your numbers, adjust your training and targets, pray together, and set the next week’s goals.
